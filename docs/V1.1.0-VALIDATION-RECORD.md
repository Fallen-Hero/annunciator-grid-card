## Editor regression correction

The first local candidate was withdrawn after the visual editor was reported not to load. Full lifecycle reproduction found an out-of-scope `action` reference accidentally placed in `_renderPanel()`, causing `setConfig()` to throw for every editor configuration. The interaction-only fields were moved back into `_interactionEditor()`. The same audit found and corrected a runtime initialization-order error where `__cellH` was read before initialization. A mounted DOM lifecycle suite and undefined-identifier scope checker are now mandatory test gates.

## Shape rendering correction

Screenshot review showed that the theme's later `.cell` rule was restoring a rectangular background, border, and shadow around Pill, Circle, Square, and Indicator Dot. Shape assembly rules now execute after theme rules and remove that outer rectangle. Square/Circle are sized from the full available short side; Indicator Dot uses 80% of that size with a 72px lower bound where space permits, compact typography, and forced text wrapping. At the reported 225×160 geometry this produces a 148px Circle/Square and a 118px Indicator Dot instead of the prior 42px cap. Paired shaped lamps also remove the rectangular parent bezel.

The border is not discarded: a theme-aware bezel is redrawn behind the lens using the same Pill, Square, Circle, or Indicator Dot geometry. Its thickness follows the configured mullion, and paired halves receive their own correctly shaped bezels.

## Illumination and flicker correction

The original translucent-illumination declaration appeared before the lens material and theme blocks, allowing those later rules to replace its shadow. Illumination now runs as the final compositing layer, after materials, themes, and shapes. Active opted-in lamps receive visible internal diffusion, a brighter translucent surface, and a color-matched halo.

Retro Flicker previously varied only a radial highlight from 90% to 100% opacity over a smooth 2.2-second cycle. It now uses irregular 1.65-second incandescent steps with a materially visible highlight/brightness range. Browser screenshots taken 420 ms apart differed at the pixel level. The effect remains limited to active Retro lamps and is disabled by `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Checkpoint 16 corrects an additional compositing conflict reported during Home Assistant testing. Retro translucent illumination previously reused the Modern broad white diffuser, creating a white center surrounded by a dark cavity. It now uses a color-filled incandescent gradient with restrained reflection. Plastic, Glass, Frosted, and Smoked receive distinct overlays, while Frosted and Smoked filters remain part of their alert keyframes.

Retro Flicker previously continued animating the hotspot while an alert independently animated the complete lens. The multiplied brightness cycles caused erratic visual beats. Blink, Pulse, Wave, Throb, Heartbeat, and Flash now take exclusive animation priority; Flicker automatically resumes after the alert becomes inactive. Wave uses a filter-based drop shadow so it no longer replaces the constant translucent inset/halo shadow. In-app Chrome sampled all six effects over time and validated standalone, material, and paired-half behavior with 63/63 checks.

## Spacer appearance and ACK rearm correction

ACK rearm now has top-level `ack_rearm_default: auto|manual` plus per-lamp `inherit|manual|auto`. ## Derived lamps, font specimens, and historical tally labels

Checkpoint 24 adds **Derived — rules only** as a first-class lamp data source. A Derived lamp has no primary Home Assistant entity and therefore never becomes INOP merely because its entity field is blank. Its explicit OFF/ON base state is evaluated first, then the existing ordered conditional-rule engine may watch any Home Assistant entity and Force ON or Force OFF while also applying severity, color, and alert behavior. Derived lamps remain normal operational panel cells: they support custom text and icons, pairs, spans, groups, interactions, ACK state, live and historical tallies, and alarm output. The editor's **+ Add derived lamp** path creates safe OFF defaults and an added rule starts as **Another entity equals on → Force ON**. Direct final-state lamp-to-lamp references are intentionally excluded so circular dependencies cannot form; two lamps can watch the same underlying Home Assistant entity instead.

The four rolling historical tally labels are now independently editable. Blank or malformed labels fall back to ALARM DAY, ALARM WEEK, ALARM MONTH, or ALARM YEAR, and the normal text-sanitization and output-escaping paths apply. Label strings do not accidentally enable a disabled tally.

Every font selector now has a live **Font preview** specimen rendered through the same resolved stack as its header, panel lamp default, or per-lamp override. Hover text exposes the exact CSS stack. Theme/default and System may still look identical when the active Home Assistant theme already uses the platform system font; Condensed, Monospace, and Serif resolve to distinct stacks. The in-app browser verified the rendered stacks, Derived OFF→ON behavior, custom historical label, ACK/tally participation, and exact no-overflow geometry at both desktop and 390×844 phone dimensions with no console errors.
